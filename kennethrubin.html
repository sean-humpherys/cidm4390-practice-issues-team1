Who Is Kenneth S. Rubin?

Kenneth S. Rubin is an author, educator, and consultant who has helped organizations understand and apply Scrum and Agile software development practices.

What Does He Know About Scrum?

Rubin is known for explaining Scrum roles, events, and artifacts, as well as how teams can use Scrum to organize their work and improve collaboration.

His book, Essential Scrum: A Practical Guide to the Most Popular Agile Process, explains Scrum concepts and provides practical guidance for teams adopting the framework.

Why Is His Work Important?

Scrum gives teams a structured way to deliver work in increments, inspect their progress, and adapt when they discover better ways to work. Rubin's explanations help readers understand these practices and apply them to real projects.
