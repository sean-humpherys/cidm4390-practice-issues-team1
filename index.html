Scrum Quick Guide

What is product backlog grooming?

Product backlog grooming, also called product backlog refinement, is the ongoing process of reviewing, clarifying, and organizing items in the Product Backlog.

What is Daily Scrum, who participates, and what is the objective? What is not Daily Scrum?

The Daily Scrum is a 15-minute Scrum event held every working day during a Sprint. Its purpose is to inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal and adapt the Sprint Backlog as necessary, creating an actionable plan for the next day of work.

Read more...

What is a Sprint Review activity?

The Sprint Review is a Scrum event held near the end of each Sprint to inspect the outcome of the Sprint and determine future adaptations. During this event, the Scrum Team presents the results of its work to key stakeholders and discusses progress toward the Product Goal.

Read more...

What is a Sprint Retrospective activity?

The Sprint Retrospective is a Scrum event held at the end of each Sprint to identify ways to improve the team's quality and effectiveness. It provides an opportunity for the Scrum Team to reflect on how the Sprint went, including collaboration, processes, tools, communication, and the Definition of Done.
