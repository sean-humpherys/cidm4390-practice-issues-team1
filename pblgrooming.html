What is product backlog grooming?

Product backlog grooming, also called product backlog refinement, is the ongoing process of reviewing, clarifying, and organizing items in the Product Backlog. The goal is to make sure that upcoming work is understood, appropriately sized, and ready for future Sprint Planning. During refinement, the Scrum Team may discuss requirements, break large Product Backlog Items (PBIs) into smaller ones, and estimate the effort needed to complete them. The Product Owner remains accountable for ordering the Product Backlog.

Backlog refinement is a collaborative activity involving the Product Owner and Developers, with the Scrum Master helping the team understand and apply Scrum effectively. The Product Owner explains priorities and desired outcomes, while Developers discuss technical considerations, dependencies, and estimates. For example, a PBI such as "Create a Scrum educational website" might be divided into smaller items for individual webpages, navigation menus, and testing. This makes the work easier to understand, estimate, and complete.

Effective backlog refinement helps reduce uncertainty and prepares the team for productive Sprint Planning. PBIs that are clear, small enough, and sufficiently understood can be considered for selection during an upcoming Sprint. Refinement does not require every detail to be finalized in advance; instead, it supports continuous learning and adaptation. For a Scrum Master, encouraging productive collaboration and helping remove obstacles to refinement can improve the team's ability to deliver valuable work.
